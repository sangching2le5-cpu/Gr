Teacher notes – Slide 3

Objective Create a need for the target grammar before presenting it.

Script “Imagine you are looking for Ben. Three people give you three different ideas. Which one is true? We don’t know! So how do we say it in English without saying it is 100% true?”

Questions

  • “Where do you think Ben is?” — accept any answer.
  • “Are you completely sure?” — students should say no.
  • “So how do we show that we are not sure?” — elicit maybe, perhaps, I think…

Expected answers “Maybe he is in his office.” / “Perhaps he is having lunch.” / “I’m not sure.”

Common mistakes Students may say “He can be in his office” — note this for later and correct gently.

Transition “English has two very useful words for this: may and might. Let’s discover them.”

Part II · May and might

Discover the meaning

Khám phá ý nghĩa – diễn tả khả năng

EnglishVietnamese
He may be in his office.
(= perhaps he is in his office)		 Có thể anh ấy đang ở văn phòng.
He might be having lunch.
(= perhaps he is having lunch)		 Có thể anh ấy đang ăn trưa.
Ask Kate. She might know.
(= perhaps she knows)		 Hãy hỏi Kate. Có thể cô ấy biết.

Key idea: We use may or might to say that something is possible — but we are not sure.

Chúng ta dùng may hoặc might để diễn tả một sự việc có khả năng đúng hoặc xảy ra, nhưng người nói chưa chắc chắn.

In the example, the speaker does not know exactly where Ben is, so he or she makes a guess.

Trong ví dụ trên, người nói không biết chính xác Ben ở đâu nên đưa ra những phỏng đoán.

Objective Establish the core meaning: possibility, not certainty.

Script “Look at the three sentences. Do they tell us a fact? No — they tell us a possibility. The speaker is guessing. In Vietnamese we say ‘có thể’. In English we say may or might.”

Questions

  • Is Ben definitely in his office? (No — maybe.)
  • Who is more sure: “Ben is in his office” or “Ben may be in his office”? (The first one.)
  • Why do we say “Ask Kate. She might know”? (Because we are not sure whether Kate knows.)

Expected answers Students should recognise the idea of uncertainty / “perhaps”.

Part II · Form

Form and sentence structure

Cấu trúc câu với may / might

Subject + may / might + V (nguyên mẫu, không có “to”)
StructureExampleVietnamese
may/might + base verb She might know. Có thể cô ấy biết.
may/might + be + adj / noun / place He may be in his office.
It may be true.		 Có thể anh ấy đang ở văn phòng.
Có thể điều đó đúng.
may/might + be + V-ing He might be having lunch. Có thể anh ấy đang ăn trưa.
may/might not + base verb She might not know. Có thể cô ấy không biết.

✔ Correct

She might know.

He may be tired.

✘ Incorrect

She might knows.

She might to know.

Sau may / might, động từ luôn ở dạng nguyên mẫu không có “to”. Không thêm -s, không thêm to.

Teacher notes – Slide 5

Objective Students can build correct affirmative and negative sentences with may/might.

Script “The rule is simple: after may or might, use the verb without ‘to’ and without ‘-s’. Say it with me: She might know. Not ‘she might knows’, not ‘she might to know’.”

Questions

  • Make a sentence about your teacher: “She may be …”
  • Make a sentence about your friend: “He might be …ing …”
  • Make it negative: “She might not …”

Common mistakes Adding -s (might knows), adding to (might to know), using can instead of may.

Transition “So we have two words — may and might. Are they different?”

Part II · Comparison

May vs. might

Hai từ có thể thay thế cho nhau

Both may and might express possibility. In most situations you can use either one:

With may

It may be true.

She may know.

With might

It might be true.

She might know.

Meaning: “Perhaps it is true.” / “Perhaps she knows.”

Hai câu có nghĩa gần như giống nhau: “Có thể điều đó đúng.” / “Có thể cô ấy biết.”

⚠ Important

Might sometimes sounds a little more tentative (dè dặt, ít chắc chắn hơn một chút) in some contexts — but do not say that might always means a lower probability than may. There is no fixed rule.

Không có quy tắc tuyệt đối rằng “might” luôn chỉ khả năng thấp hơn “may”. Trong hầu hết trường hợp, hai từ này thay thế cho nhau được.

Oral practice / Luyện nói nhanh:

Say each sentence twice — once with may, once with might.

1. It ___ be true.   2. She ___ know the answer.   3. They ___ be at home.

Teacher notes – Slide 6

Objective Students understand that may and might are largely interchangeable.

Script “Here is good news: you do not have to choose. May and might are brothers. Might can sound a little softer or more careful, but both are correct.”

Activity Point at individual students and ask them to transform: teacher says “It may be true” → student says “It might be true.” Do five or six quick rounds. Keep the pace fast.

Questions

  • “Is ‘It might be true’ stronger or weaker than ‘It may be true’?” (Practically the same; might can feel slightly more tentative.)

Common mistakes Students may believe might = 10% and may = 50%. Correct this gently.

Transition “Now let’s look at the negative — because the negative is very useful.”

Part II · Form

Positive and negative forms

Dạng khẳng định và phủ định

Subject + may / might + V
Subject + may / might not + V

✔ Positive – khẳng định

It may be true.Có thể điều đó đúng.

She might know.Có thể cô ấy biết.

✘ Negative – phủ định

It may not be true.Có thể điều đó không đúng.

She might not know.Có thể cô ấy không biết.

Meaning difference:

It may be true. = perhaps it is true → có thể đúng

It may not be true. = perhaps it isn’t true → có thể không đúng

Chúng ta thêm not sau may/might để diễn tả khả năng một sự việc không xảy ra hoặc không đúng.

Quick check / Kiểm tra nhanh: Is the speaker sure?

1. “She might not know.”   2. “It may not be true.”   3. “She doesn’t know.”

Câu 1 và 2: người nói không chắc. Câu 3: người nói khẳng định một sự thật.

Teacher notes – Slide 7

Objective Students can form and understand negative possibility sentences.

Script “Be careful! ‘She might not know’ does NOT mean ‘She doesn’t know’. It means ‘Perhaps she doesn’t know — I’m not sure.’ The negative is still only a possibility.”

Questions

  • “Your friend didn’t answer the phone. Give me a negative possibility.” (She might not have her phone.)
  • “Is ‘It may not be true’ the same as ‘It is not true’?” (No.)

Common mistakes Students confuse may not with a definite negative. Emphasise: may not = “perhaps not”.

Transition “There is one small spelling trap: may be and maybe. Let’s fix it now.”

Part II · Spelling & usage

May be vs. maybe

Hai từ – một từ

may be (2 words)

may = modal verb  |  be = base verb

It may be true.Điều đó có thể đúng.

She may be tired.Có thể cô ấy đang mệt.

→ Đây là cấu trúc động từ: may + be.

maybe (1 word)

maybe = adverb (= perhaps)

“Is it true?” “Maybe. I’m not sure.”“Điều đó có đúng không?” “Có thể. Tôi không chắc.”

Maybe she is tired.Có lẽ cô ấy đang mệt.

→ Đây là trạng từ, thường đứng đầu câu.

Compare:

She may be tired. — may + be + adjective

Maybe she is tired. — adverb + full sentence

Hai câu có nghĩa gần giống nhau nhưng cấu trúc ngữ pháp khác nhau.

Classroom activity: Which is correct — may be or maybe?

1. “___ he is at home.”   2. “He ___ at home.”   3. “___ I will see you tomorrow.”   4. “It ___ raining outside.”

1. Maybe  |  2. may be  |  3. Maybe  |  4. may be

1 và 3: đứng đầu câu, là trạng từ → Maybe. 2 và 4: sau chủ ngữ, là động từ khuyết thiếu + be → may be.

Teacher notes – Slide 8

Objective Students never confuse the two-word verb form with the one-word adverb.

Script “This is a very common writing mistake, even for good students. May be — two words — is a verb. Maybe — one word — is an adverb, like ‘perhaps’. Test: if you can replace it with ‘perhaps’, write one word.”

Questions

  • “Can I write ‘Maybe he may be late’?” (Grammatically possible but redundant; better: “He may be late.”)
  • Where does maybe usually go in a sentence? (At the beginning.)

Common mistakes “Maybe be he is busy.” — wrong; it is either “Maybe he is busy.” or “He may be busy.”

Transition “So far, everything is about now and the future. What about yesterday? Let’s look at the timeline.”

Part III · Past possibility

Present or past? The timeline

Hiện tại hay quá khứ?

Now

He might be at home.

Có thể bây giờ anh ấy đang ở nhà.

Now · in progress

He might be sleeping.

Có thể anh ấy đang ngủ.

Past

He might have left.

Có thể anh ấy đã đi rồi.

Past · in progress

He might have been sleeping when I called.

Có thể anh ấy đang ngủ vào lúc tôi gọi.

Rule of thumb: If the guess is about the past, we add have + V3 after may/might.

Nếu phỏng đoán về quá khứ, ta thêm have + V3 (quá khứ phân từ) sau may/might.

Teacher notes – Slide 9

Objective Students see the grammatical change visually across time.

Script “Look at the timeline. The first two boxes are about now. The last two are about the past. Do you see the difference? In the past we add two words: have + the third form of the verb.”

Questions

  • “He might be sleeping” — is this about now or yesterday? (Now.)
  • “He might have been sleeping” — now or yesterday? (The past.)
  • What is the difference between “might have left” and “might have been sleeping”?

Expected answers “might have left” = a finished past action; “might have been sleeping” = an action in progress at a past moment.

Transition “Let’s look at the past structures in detail.”

Part III · Form

May have … / Might have …

Possibility in the past – khả năng trong quá khứ

Subject + may / might + have + V3 (past participle)
Subject + may / might not + have + V3
EnglishVietnamese
She may have been asleep.
(= perhaps she was asleep)		 Có thể cô ấy đã ngủ.
You might have left it at work.
(= perhaps you left it at work)		 Có thể bạn đã để quên nó ở chỗ làm.
She might not have known about it.
(= perhaps she didn’t know)		 Có thể cô ấy đã không biết về cuộc họp.
He may not have been feeling well.
(= perhaps he wasn’t feeling well)		 Có thể hôm qua anh ấy đã cảm thấy không khỏe.

Note: In this structure, have is not “possession” — it is part of the perfect structure. It never changes to has.

Trong cấu trúc này, have không mang nghĩa sở hữu mà là một phần của cấu trúc hoàn thành. Nó không bao giờ đổi thành has.

Teacher notes – Slide 10

Objective Students can form past possibility sentences correctly.

Script “Read the four sentences. Notice: may have been, might have left, might not have known, may not have been feeling. In every one, we have have + the third form.”

Questions

  • “Kate didn’t answer her phone. What do you think?” → “She may have been asleep.”
  • “I can’t find my phone.” → “You might have left it at work.”
  • “Why wasn’t Amy at the meeting?” → “She might not have known about it.”

Common mistakes “She may has been asleep.” / “She might have be asleep.” / “He may have went.” Watch for these and correct on the spot.

Transition “Let’s look more closely at the different past forms.”

Part III · Form

Different past structures

Các dạng cấu trúc quá khứ

StructureExampleVietnamese
might have + V3 He might have left. Có thể anh ấy đã đi rồi.
might have + been + adj She might have been asleep. Có thể cô ấy đã ngủ.
might have + been + V-ing He might have been working. Có thể lúc đó anh ấy đang làm việc.
might not have + V3 He might not have heard. Có thể anh ấy đã không nghe thấy.

Irregular verbs you need / Động từ bất quy tắc cần nhớ

go – went – gone

leave – left – left

know – knew – known

tell – told – told

forget – forgot – forgotten

hear – heard – heard

Với động từ có quy tắc, thêm -ed để tạo quá khứ phân từ (want → wanted). Với động từ bất quy tắc, cần học dạng V3.

Teacher notes – Slide 11

Objective Students recognise the three past patterns and recall key irregular V3 forms.

Script “Three patterns only. Number one: might have left — a simple past action. Number two: might have been asleep — a past state. Number three: might have been working — a past action in progress.”

Activity Quick drill: teacher says the base verb, students say the V3. go → gone, leave → left, know → known, tell → told, forget → forgotten, hear → heard.

Common mistakes “might have went” (should be gone), “might have heared” (should be heard).

Transition “Let’s practise. Present or past?”

Part III · Guided practice

Guided practice: Present or past?

Xác định thời gian và cấu trúc

Look at each sentence. Decide: is the guess about now or about the past? Which structure is used?

Hãy xác định mỗi câu phỏng đoán về hiện tại hay quá khứ, và cấu trúc được dùng là gì.

  • 1. He might be sleeping.

    Present · in progress → might + be + V-ing

    Phỏng đoán một hành động đang diễn ra ở hiện tại: “Có thể anh ấy đang ngủ.”

  • 2. She may have left already.

    Past → may + have + V3 (left)

    Phỏng đoán về quá khứ: “Có thể cô ấy đã đi rồi.”

  • 3. They might not know the answer.

    Present · negative → might not + base verb

    Phỏng đoán ở hiện tại, dạng phủ định: “Có thể họ không biết câu trả lời.”

  • 4. You might have been driving too fast.

    Past · in progress → might + have + been + V-ing

    Phỏng đoán một hành động đang diễn ra trong quá khứ: “Có thể lúc đó bạn đã lái xe quá nhanh.”

  • 5. I may not have heard you.

    Past · negative → may not + have + V3 (heard)

    “Có thể tôi đã không nghe thấy bạn.”

  • 6. It could be true.

    Present → could + base verb

    “Điều đó có thể đúng.” — could cũng diễn tả khả năng, giống may/might.

Giáo viên: cho học sinh trả lời miệng trước, sau đó mới bấm “Show answer”.

Part IV · Could

Could – similar to may and might

Could cũng diễn tả khả năng

Present / future possibility

It’s a strange story, but it could be true.

Đó là một câu chuyện kỳ lạ, nhưng nó có thể đúng.

(= it is possible that it’s true)

Past possibility

You could have left your phone at work.

Có thể bạn đã để quên điện thoại ở chỗ làm.

(= it’s possible that you left it there)

Subject + could + V   →   possibility now / in the future
Subject + could have + V3   →   possibility in the past

Trong các ví dụ này, could diễn tả khả năng, tương tự may và might.

could have + V3 dùng để phỏng đoán một sự việc có thể đã xảy ra trong quá khứ.

⚠ But be careful with the negative!

couldn’t is NOT the same as may not / might not. We will study this on the next slide.

Teacher notes – Slide 13

Objective Students see that could is a third way to express possibility.

Script “Good news again: you already know could for ability — ‘I could swim when I was five.’ But could has a second job: possibility. ‘It could be true’ = ‘It may be true’ = ‘It might be true’.”

Questions

  • “It could rain later.” — is the speaker sure? (No.)
  • “You could have left your keys in the car.” — now or past? (Past.)

Common mistakes Students may think could is only about ability. Give one clear example of each meaning.

Transition “Now the most important slide of this lesson: might not have vs. couldn’t have.”

Part IV · Key contrast

Might not have vs. couldn’t have

Điểm ngữ pháp quan trọng nhất của bài

might not have + V3

Why hasn’t Sarah replied to my message? I suppose she might not have received it.

Tại sao Sarah chưa trả lời tin nhắn của tôi? Tôi cho rằng có thể cô ấy đã không nhận được tin nhắn.

(= it’s possible that she didn’t receive it — perhaps she did, perhaps she didn’t)

Người nói chưa biết chắc: có thể cô ấy nhận được, cũng có thể không.

couldn’t have + V3

Sarah couldn’t have received my message. Otherwise she would have replied.

Sarah không thể nào đã nhận được tin nhắn của tôi. Nếu không, cô ấy đã trả lời rồi.

(= it is not possible that she got my message)

Người nói cho rằng điều đó không thể xảy ra, dựa trên suy luận.

Remember:

might not have + V3 = có thể đã không xảy ra (người nói chưa chắc chắn)

couldn’t have + V3 = không thể nào đã xảy ra (người nói kết luận chắc chắn là không thể)

Vì vậy, không nên nhầm lẫn couldn’t have + V3 với might not have + V3.

Teacher notes – Slide 14

Objective Students distinguish an uncertain negative from a confident negative deduction.

Script “Look at the two sentences about Sarah. Same situation, but two different meanings. In the green column, the speaker is guessing: ‘Perhaps she didn’t get it.’ In the red column, the speaker is sure: ‘It is impossible that she got it — because she would have replied.’”

Questions

  • In which sentence is the speaker more certain? (couldn’t have)
  • Can we say “She couldn’t have received it, but perhaps she did”? (No — it is contradictory.)
  • Give me a reason why someone might not have received a message. (Phone was off, no internet, wrong number…)

Common mistakes Using couldn’t have when the speaker is only guessing, and using might not have when the speaker has strong evidence.

Transition “Let’s test your understanding.”

Part IV · Check

Check understanding

Chọn dạng đúng và giải thích lý do

Choose: may havemight not havecouldn’t have

Hãy giải thích lựa chọn của em bằng lời trước khi giáo viên hiện đáp án.

  • 1. Amy wasn’t at the meeting. Perhaps she didn’t know about it. → She ________ about it.

    might not have known

    Người nói chỉ phỏng đoán, không khẳng định chắc chắn Amy không biết → dùng might not have + V3.

  • 2. The police say the fire was deliberate. It ________ an accident.

    couldn’t have been

    Cảnh sát kết luận vụ cháy là cố ý → “không thể nào là tai nạn”. Đây là suy luận chắc chắn, dùng couldn’t have + V3.

  • 3. I can’t find my keys. Perhaps I left them in the car. → I ________ them in the car.

    Phỏng đoán về quá khứ, khả năng xảy ra → may have + V3 (leave – left – left).

  • 4. Tom didn’t come to the party. Perhaps he didn’t want to come. → He ________ to come.

    might not have wanted

    Đây chỉ là một khả năng → might not have + V3 (want – wanted – wanted).

  • 5. Mike says he tried to find you yesterday. But you were in your office all day. → He ________ very hard.

    couldn’t have tried

    Người nói suy luận chắc chắn: nếu Mike cố gắng thì đã tìm thấy → “không thể nào đã cố gắng nhiều” → couldn’t have + V3.

Exercise 29.1 – Answers & explanations

Đáp án và giải thích

  • 1. A: Do you know where Helen is? B: I’m not sure. She might be in her room.

    might be in her room

    “Có thể cô ấy đang ở trong phòng.” — might + be + cụm chỉ nơi chốn, phỏng đoán vị trí hiện tại.

  • 2. A: Is there a bookshop near here? B: I’m not sure, but ask Anna. She might know.

    might know

    “Có thể cô ấy biết.” — might + động từ nguyên mẫu know (không dùng knows, không dùng dạng tiếp diễn).

  • 3. A: Where are those people from? B: I don’t know. They might be Brazilian.

    might be Brazilian

    “Có thể họ là người Brazil.” — might + be + tính từ chỉ quốc tịch, dùng để phỏng đoán.

  • 4. A: I hope you can help me. B: I’ll try, but it may not be possible.

    may not be possible

    “Có thể điều đó không khả thi.” — may not + be + tính từ, diễn tả khả năng một việc không thể thực hiện được.

  • 5. A: Whose phone is this? B: It’s not mine. It may be Tom’s.

    may be Tom’s

    “Có thể nó là của Tom.” — Tom’s là dạng sở hữu, thay cho Tom’s phone.

  • 6. A: Why doesn’t George answer his phone? B: He might be driving.

    might be driving

    “Có thể anh ấy đang lái xe.” — might + be + V-ing diễn tả hành động đang diễn ra, giải thích vì sao George không nghe máy.

  • 7. A: Do you know anyone who has a key to this cupboard? B: Rachel might have one, but I’m not sure.

    might have one

    “Có thể Rachel có một chiếc.” — one thay cho a key, tránh lặp lại danh từ.

  • 8. A: Gary is in a strange mood today. B: Yes, he is. He may not be feeling well.

    may not be feeling well

    “Có thể anh ấy đang cảm thấy không khỏe.” — may not + be + V-ing, phỏng đoán trạng thái ở hiện tại.

Exercise 29.2 – Answers & explanations

Đáp án và giải thích

  • 1. He might be having lunch.

    be having

    Might + be + V-ing: hành động đang diễn ra ở hiện tại.

  • 2. It may be her brother.

    be

    Sau may dùng động từ nguyên mẫu be.

  • 3. She may not have arrived yet.

    have arrived

    May not + have + V3: khả năng một việc chưa xảy ra tính đến hiện tại. V3 của arrive là arrived.

  • 4. He might be waiting outside.

    be waiting

    Phỏng đoán hành động đang diễn ra ở hiện tại → might be + V-ing.

  • 5. Sam may have told him.

    have told

    Sự việc đã xảy ra trước thời điểm John biết → may have + V3. tell – told – told.

  • 6. He might have gone home.

    have gone

    Phỏng đoán về hành động đã xảy ra. V3 của go là gone.

  • 7. She might be watching TV.

    be watching

    Câu hỏi “What’s she doing?” hỏi về hành động hiện tại → might be + V-ing.

  • 8. He may have a younger sister.

    have

    Diễn tả sự sở hữu ở hiện tại → may + have (nguyên mẫu). Không dùng have had.

  • 9. You may have left it in the restaurant last night.

    have left

    “Last night” chỉ thời gian quá khứ → may have + V3. leave – left – left.

  • 10. He might not have heard the doorbell.

    have heard

    Phỏng đoán lý do trong quá khứ → might not have + V3. hear – heard – heard.

  • 11. She may have forgotten.

    have forgotten

    Việc quên có thể đã xảy ra trước thời điểm nói → may have + V3. forget – forgot – forgotten.

Exercise 29.3 – might not have … or couldn’t have …

Chọn dạng phủ định đúng

might not have + V3

The speaker is not sure.

Người nói chưa chắc chắn — chỉ là một khả năng.

couldn’t have + V3

The speaker is sure it is impossible.

Người nói cho rằng điều đó không thể xảy ra.

  • 1. A: I was surprised Amy wasn’t at the meeting. Perhaps she didn’t know about it. B: Maybe. She ________ about it.

  • 2. A: I wonder why Tom didn’t come to the party. Perhaps he didn’t want to come. B: It’s possible. He ________ to come.

  • 3. A: I wonder how the fire started. Was it an accident? B: No, the police say it ________ an accident. It was deliberate.

  • 4. A: Mike says he needs to see you. He tried to find you yesterday. B: Well, he ________ very hard. I was in my office all day.

Teacher notes – Slide 20

Objective Students choose between an uncertain negative and an impossible negative.

Script “For each sentence, ask yourself one question: is the speaker guessing or is the speaker sure? If they are guessing, use might not have. If they have evidence and are sure it is impossible, use couldn’t have.”

Clues

  • Words like perhaps, maybe, it’s possible, I’m not sure → might not have.
  • Words like the police say, a reason or logical conclusion → couldn’t have.

Exercise 29.3 – Answers & explanations

Đáp án và giải thích

  • 1. She might not have known about it.

    might not have known

    Người nói chỉ đưa ra phỏng đoán (“Perhaps she didn’t know”), không khẳng định chắc chắn → might not have + V3.

  • 2. He might not have wanted to come.

    might not have wanted

    Đây là một khả năng về mong muốn trong quá khứ → might not have + V3 (want – wanted – wanted).

  • 3. The police say it couldn’t have been an accident. It was deliberate.

    couldn’t have been

    Couldn’t have been thể hiện sự phủ định mạnh: theo thông tin cảnh sát đưa ra, vụ cháy không thể nào là tai nạn.

  • 4. Well, he couldn’t have tried very hard. I was in my office all day.

    couldn’t have tried

    Người nói suy luận: nếu Mike thực sự cố gắng thì anh ấy đã tìm thấy mình → “không thể nào đã cố gắng nhiều”.

  • 5. No, I’m not sure. He might not have been American.

    might not have been American

    Người nói không chắc về quốc tịch của người đàn ông → dùng might not have been. Không dùng couldn’t have been vì chưa có cơ sở để kết luận điều đó là không thể.

Remember the difference: might not have = có thể đã không (chưa chắc) · couldn’t have = không thể nào đã (kết luận chắc chắn).

Part VI · Accuracy

Error correction

Sửa lỗi và giải thích quy tắc

Each sentence has a mistake. Correct it and explain the rule.

Mỗi câu có một lỗi. Hãy sửa và giải thích quy tắc.

  • 1. She might goes home early.

    She might go home early.

    Sau might, động từ ở dạng nguyên mẫu — không thêm -s.

  • 2. He may to be tired.

    He may be tired.

    Sau may không dùng to + động từ. Dùng động từ nguyên mẫu không “to”.

  • 3. They might have went home.

    They might have gone home.

    Sau have phải dùng V3 (quá khứ phân từ). go – went – gone.

  • 4. She couldn’t have received it, but perhaps she did.

    She might not have received it, but perhaps she did.

    Câu này tự mâu thuẫn: couldn’t have nghĩa là “không thể nào đã xảy ra”, nên không thể nói tiếp “but perhaps she did”. Nếu muốn diễn tả khả năng, phải dùng might not have.

  • 5. Maybe be he is busy.

    Maybe he is busy. / He may be busy.

    Không dùng maybe và be cùng lúc theo cách này. Hoặc dùng trạng từ maybe + câu đầy đủ, hoặc dùng may be + tính từ.
